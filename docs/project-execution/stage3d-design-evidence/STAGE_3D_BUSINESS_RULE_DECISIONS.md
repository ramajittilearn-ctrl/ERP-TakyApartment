# Stage 3D business-rule decisions

**Status:** No Stage 3D policy in this document is Owner-approved. Recommendations are defaults for review, not authorization to implement or process real money.

| ID | Owner decision | Recommended default | Rationale and consequence if different | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D3D-01 | Which methods may operators record? | Cash and bank transfer. Add QR only if the Owner confirms that QR settles into a traceable bank account and selects its evidence rule. | The user brief names cash and transfer. Avoid representing QR as verified when the app has no bank integration. | Open |
| D3D-02 | Are partial payments allowed? May an invoice have multiple payments? | Yes. Allow any positive amount up to the current outstanding balance, and allow repeated payments until zero. | This fits the existing payment screen concept and supports ordinary rent collection. If disallowed, simplify confirmation to exact balance and reject smaller amounts. | Open |
| D3D-03 | Can one payment cover multiple invoices? | Yes, only for invoices on the same lease, with each allocation explicitly shown before confirmation. | Avoid cross-tenant allocation and implicit matching. If the Owner wants one payment per invoice, simplify allocation and receipt line items. | Open |
| D3D-04 | What happens to overpayments? | Reject the payment allocation and require the operator to enter the amount actually assigned to invoices. Do not create credit. | This matches the existing Stage 3 plan's overpayment-rejection test and avoids adding a tenant-credit liability ledger. A credit policy needs refund, expiry, transfer, and reporting rules. | Open |
| D3D-05 | Who may confirm receipt of funds? | STAFF may create a pending entry and attach evidence. ADMIN alone confirms, reverses, and issues or cancels receipts. | Separation of entry and settlement confirmation limits accidental or unsupported receipts. Approval of STAFF confirmation would reduce owner workload for a 15-room property but weakens separation of duties. | Open |
| D3D-06 | What does the payment date mean? | Record the actual date/time funds were received, separately from system `createdAt` and `confirmedAt`. Display the date in Asia/Bangkok. | Backdated entry should preserve both the effective date and the actual record/confirmation times. Decide whether operators need a date only or a time as well. | Open |
| D3D-07 | What evidence is needed? | Require a transfer reference or private slip for bank transfer where available. Do not require evidence for cash. Never treat an attachment as proof of settlement by itself. | The existing `Attachment` metadata supports payment linkage, but no payment upload/download API was found. Owner must approve mandatory evidence, accepted file types, file-size cap, retention, and access. | Open |
| D3D-08 | How should duplicate payments be blocked? | Enforce idempotency for every command. For transfer, block duplicate bank reference within the selected receiving account. For cash, block confirmation on a likely duplicate until ADMIN reviews it. ADMIN may override only with a reason; record the reviewer, reason, and candidate payment IDs in audit. | A global reference uniqueness rule can be too broad. Cash has no reliable external key, so exact duplicate prevention cannot distinguish two legitimate equal cash payments without an audited review. | Open |
| D3D-09 | When is a receipt issued? | Atomically with ADMIN confirmation and allocation. Pending transfers have no receipt. | This keeps the receipt aligned to confirmed money and avoids separate states where the payment is confirmed but its receipt was not issued. If confirmation is STAFF-enabled, the Owner must decide whether it also issues the receipt. | Open |
| D3D-10 | What receipt number format and sequence? | `REC-YYYYMM-NNNN`, with a dedicated monthly counter, globally unique across properties, never reused after cancellation. | This follows the Stage 3C invoice numbering pattern while keeping receipt numbers in a separate namespace. The Owner must approve the prefix, period basis, and annual rollover expectation. | Open |
| D3D-11 | How may a receipt be cancelled? | ADMIN only, with reason. Cancellation requires the linked payment to be reversed in the same transaction; retain the cancelled receipt and number. | Preserves an audit trail and prevents a cancelled receipt from leaving a payment counted as settled. Decide whether cancellation ever may occur without a payment reversal. | Open |
| D3D-12 | What correction is permitted? | Reverse the full payment, then record a corrected payment. Do not edit confirmed amounts or allocations. | Full reversal keeps receipts simple and immutable. Partial reversals require proportional allocation and receipt rules. | Open |
| D3D-13 | How are payments assigned across old balances? | Require explicit invoice selection. If the Owner approves automatic allocation, use oldest due date first, then invoice number as a stable tie-break. | Explicit selection avoids surprising allocation to a prior invoice. Automatic priority is a policy decision and needs a visible preview. | Open |
| D3D-14 | What if money arrives after an invoice is VOID? | Never allocate it to the VOID invoice. Record it as an unresolved exception with no active invoice allocation, no effect on rent balances, and no receipt. Let ADMIN resolve it only by an explicit, audited allocation to an eligible issued invoice or by recording a refund with evidence. If the Owner does not approve this exception workflow, keep the case outside Stage 3D and require a documented operator procedure. | Stage 3C revision atomically voids and replaces an invoice, and current invoice commands do not inspect payment state. The system must retain visibility of received funds without falsely treating them as settled rent. Exception resolution, refund evidence, and liability reporting need explicit policy. | Open |
| D3D-15 | Are deposits accepted in this workflow? | No. Keep deposits in `SecurityDeposit` and outside payment allocations and rental balance. | The current deposit model is a separate liability stream and has no audited receipt/refund lifecycle. Deposit posting requires a dedicated approved ledger design. | Open |
| D3D-16 | Receipt content and language | Thai, A4 portrait, baht with two decimals, tenant and room labels from the frozen invoice snapshot, invoice allocations, method, payment date, reference, receipt number, issuer, and issue timestamp. | The frozen invoice stores historical labels. Owner should approve legal entity/property text, address, tax identifiers, and required wording before PDF layout work. | Open |
| D3D-17 | Do historic payments get migrated or mapped? | No automatic migration. Inventory read-only, preserve source rows, then approve an explicit mapping and reconciliation plan separately. | Stage 3C explicitly blocks inferred legacy invoice mappings. Existing payment rows may be demo or unmapped and cannot be presumed valid operational settlements. | Open |
| D3D-18 | When do pending payment entries expire? | Pending entries do not block invoice void or revision because they have no invoice allocation. Require ADMIN review of aged pending entries; do not expire them automatically in the first release. | Automatic expiry can silently discard an unresolved transfer or cash receipt. Stale entries need an operator queue, but they must not stop the invoice lifecycle. Approve an expiry duration and notification process if the queue burden is unacceptable. | Open |
| D3D-19 | What PDF delivery and retention behavior is required? | Provide a Thai A4 print view and browser Save as PDF from the immutable snapshot. Do not retain a separate PDF binary in the first release. | This avoids a new server PDF and file-retention service. If the Owner needs byte-identical archival copies, approve a server PDF renderer, storage, checksum, and backup requirements. | Open |

## Decisions that block implementation

At minimum, D3D-02, D3D-04, D3D-05, D3D-08, D3D-09, D3D-11, D3D-13, D3D-14, D3D-15, and D3D-18 need explicit Owner answers because they change stored states, authorization, invoice lifecycle behavior, or financial outcomes. Receipt identity and content decisions D3D-10, D3D-16, and D3D-19 are also needed before PDF and numbering implementation.

The recommendations provide a coherent starting configuration. They do not count as approval. If the Owner chooses another rule, update the requirements and technical design before implementation starts.
